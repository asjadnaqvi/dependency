Network

Global importance of suppliers

How to read: Higher scores mean a supplier is more important in the global product network, not necessarily a larger source of our imports. Rows show our top 10 suppliers by import value.

Direct / Total: Initial HITS score (iteration 1) / refined score (iteration 30). Compare suppliers within columns; these scores do not measure indirect shipments or substitutability.

Scale and risk: Raw shows hub scores; Normalized (%) shows their shares across all our suppliers, not import shares. Risk-adjusted incorporates country risk. Network display filters and link colors do not affect either heatplot.

Import Dependency

How to read: Higher Direct values mean more concentrated sourcing. Rows are ranked by Direct, highest first. In normalized mode, Total above Direct indicates greater relative exposure after accounting for network importance.

Direct / Total: Actual import share / import-share fraction times the supplier's network score at iteration 30. Direct is unchanged by risk weighting; Risk-adjusted uses the risk-weighted network score for Total.

Scale and risk: Raw Direct is a fraction (0.40 = 40%); Total is raw exposure. Normalized Total is exposure share across all suppliers, not actual import share or absolute risk. Total does not measure indirect imports or disruption probability. Missing risk uses multiplier 1 and triggers a warning.

NRAI

Normalized risk amplification index across direct and indirect trade links.
Initial compares risk-weighted and trade-only pairwise scores at iteration 1; Refined compares them at iteration 30. These are network-score ratios, not measured indirect trade flows.
Values above 1 indicate risk amplification, values below 1 indicate risk dampening, and 1 indicates no net change.

Country and supplier details

Country: Country code and name. Product exports to focus: Bilateral product trade in source units. Import share: Share of the focus country's product imports. Exporter risk: Country risk score (0-1); missing values use multiplier 1.

Trade-only hub (1) / (30): Initial / refined global network importance. Risk-adjusted hub (1) / (30): The same scores with country-risk weighting. Trade-only / Risk-adjusted exposure: Import-share fraction times the corresponding iteration-30 hub score.

Global importance - Direct / Total: Initial / refined hub scores under the selected weighting. Import Dependency - Direct / Total: Import share / network-adjusted exposure under the selected weighting. These four columns match the heatplots.

Raw shows scores and import-share fractions; Normalized (%) uses all suppliers as denominators. Full-country hub columns need not sum to 100%. Non-suppliers have zero bilateral exports, import share and exposure.
